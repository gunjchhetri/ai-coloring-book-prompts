Each one is ready to open and print well before October.

Tips for halloween activity book

  • Publish by early September; Halloween search traffic builds through the whole month before.
  • For ages 3-6, say 'smiling' and 'friendly' about every ghost, bat and witch; scary faces get one-star reviews.
  • A 24-30 page book printed at home makes a cheap, candy-free class party favor.
