Tips for large print word search

  • Keep words familiar and cheerful; seniors buy word searches to relax, and a theme like gardening or old songs brings back good memories.
  • Give 10-15 words per list in the brief so each puzzle has a clear subtopic, like Herbs or Garden Birds.
  • Black and white ink only; it keeps the print cost low and the letters crisp for tired eyes.
