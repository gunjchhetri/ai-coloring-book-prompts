Tips for ocean activity book

  • Name real sea animals (octopus, sea turtle, clownfish, manta ray) so every page shows a different creature.
  • Ocean books sell best from May to August; time the launch for beach season.
  • For older kids, use reef science words like PLANKTON and SALINITY; parents and teachers love a puzzle that teaches.
