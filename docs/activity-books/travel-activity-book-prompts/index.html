Tips for travel activity book

  • Pick black and white for older kids' puzzles; pencils and pens work on them and they print cheaply for home use before a trip.
  • Skip activities that need scissors or glue; everything here works with one pencil on a moving seat.
  • Use window-spotting words in the word search (BRIDGE, TUNNEL, COW) so kids connect the puzzle to the trip.
