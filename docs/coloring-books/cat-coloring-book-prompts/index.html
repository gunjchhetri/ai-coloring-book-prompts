Tips for cat coloring book

  • Cats in cozy settings (window seats, bookshelves, plants, teacups) are a strong adult and teen sub-niche, often sold as gifts for cat owners.
  • For kids, give the cats jobs and hobbies, like baking, gardening or reading, so each page tells a tiny story.
  • Adult cat books perform well at 40-50 pages with a premium finish, priced as a gift.
