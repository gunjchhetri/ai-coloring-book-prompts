Tips for dinosaur coloring book

  • Keep toddler dinosaur books friendly and never scary: one big, smiling dinosaur per page sells better to parents of 2-5 year olds than roaring predators.
  • Mix the famous species (T-rex, triceratops, stegosaurus) with lesser-known ones like ankylosaurus and parasaurolophus so the Look Inside preview shows real variety.
  • Dinosaurs doing everyday kid things, like birthdays, bath time or school, is a proven KDP sub-niche that stands apart from plain fossil books.
