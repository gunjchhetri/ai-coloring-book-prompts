Tips for stress relief coloring book

  • Lean on slow, quiet subjects (still water, tea, sleeping animals, rain on a window); calm scenes beat busy ones for this buyer.
  • Repeating patterns such as waves, scales and pebbles are the most soothing pages to color; mix several pattern pages into a scene book.
  • Keep pages free of words; many anxiety-relief buyers find affirmations distracting and prefer to let the picture do the work.
