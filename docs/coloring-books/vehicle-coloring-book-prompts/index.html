Tips for vehicle coloring book

  • Construction vehicles are a strong sub-niche on their own; a named-machine book helps kids learn the difference between an excavator and a backhoe.
  • Toddlers want one big vehicle per page doing its job, like a dump truck tipping dirt, not crowded traffic scenes.
  • Classic cars and steam trains reach teen and adult buyers, often as gifts for car enthusiasts.
