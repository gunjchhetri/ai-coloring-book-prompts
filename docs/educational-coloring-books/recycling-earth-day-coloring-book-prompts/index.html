Tips for recycling and earth day coloring book

  • Publish or print in March; Earth Day searches peak in the weeks before April 22.
  • Keep the habits practical (turn off the tap while brushing) so kids can do them the same day.
  • Avoid scary pictures of polluted oceans for little kids; show the fix, not the disaster.
