Can you sell AI coloring books on Amazon KDP?

Yes. Amazon KDP accepts books with AI-generated images, as long as you disclose it and the book is original and worth buying. Here is what that means in practice, and how the prompts in this library are written to fit.

The rules that matter

  • Disclose AI content. When you publish, KDP asks whether the text, images or translation are AI-generated. Answer yes for AI images, even if you edited them. Books that are only AI-assisted (you used AI to brainstorm or edit your own work) do not need disclosure. See KDP's content guidelines.
  • Use only original characters. No characters, logos or names from films, games, toys or shows, and no real people. That is a trademark and copyright problem whether or not AI drew it. Every prompt here uses original characters.
  • Fewer, better books. KDP limits how many new titles one account can create each week (KDP announcement), so a cohesive book with a clear audience beats a pile of quick ones.

What sells

  • A clear reader. "Coloring book for toddlers" competes with everyone. "Big and easy farm animal coloring book for ages 2-4" finds its buyer.
  • A theme that holds together. Thirty pages that belong in one book, not thirty unrelated images.
  • The right line weight for the age. Bold, simple shapes for little hands; detail for teens and adults. InkChamps sets this from the age band on every prompt.
  • Single-sided pages. Coloring books print one picture per sheet so markers don't bleed through.
  • Enough pages. KDP paperbacks need at least 24 pages; most coloring books sell at 30 to 50.

Sizes and covers

8.5 × 11 in is the standard coloring and activity book size; 8.5 × 8.5 in suits picture books and toddler books. You will also need a cover with the spine sized to your page count. See the KDP cover prompts.
