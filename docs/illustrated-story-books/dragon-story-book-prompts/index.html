Tips for dragon story book

  • Give the dragon one fixed colour and one small detail (teal scales, a chipped horn) so it stays the same on every page.
  • Flip the expected: a gentle, scared or silly dragon is more memorable than a fierce one.
  • Colored pencil and hand-drawn styles show dragon scales and fire well; save comic style for the funny ones.
