Tips for coloring book cover

  • Show two or three sample pages' worth of characters on the front; buyers want to see the style they are paying for.
  • Count sheets, not pages: a coloring book printed on one side has as many sheets as coloring pages.
  • Use high contrast and big shapes; the cover must still read at thumbnail size on a phone.
