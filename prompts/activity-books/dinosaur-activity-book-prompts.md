## 1. Friendly dinosaur valley for preschoolers

**Ages:** 3-6 · **Pages:** 24 · **Size:** 8.5 × 11 in · **Activities:** Connect the dots (6), Shadow matching (6), Mazes (6), Coloring pages (6) · **Maze styles:** tube, square tube · **Quality:** Standard · **Credits:** 24 Standard · 48 Premium

```text
24 pages of friendly dinosaurs in a sunny prehistoric valley: 6 dot-to-dots, 6 shadow matches, 6 chunky mazes and 6 coloring pages. A baby triceratops hatching from an egg, a brachiosaurus eating treetop leaves, a stegosaurus splashing in a puddle, a pteranodon gliding over a smoking volcano, a little T. rex digging up a bone. Mazes help a hungry dinosaur reach its nest of eggs or a pile of ferns. Every dinosaur is smiling and gentle, with no fighting or sharp teeth.
```

## 2. Dinosaur puzzle adventure for ages 6-9

```text
24 pages of a dinosaur expedition through jungles, swamps, volcano fields and a riverbank full of footprints: 6 mazes, 5 word searches, 5 spot the differences, 4 dot-to-dots and 4 coloring pages. Mazes guide a raptor back to its nest or an explorer jeep through ferns. Word search words include DINOSAUR, HORNS, PLATES, RAPTOR, FOSSIL, VOLCANO, JURASSIC, HERBIVORE, CARNIVORE, SKELETON and FOOTPRINT. ## 3. Fossil hunter logic book for ages 9-13
```

**Ages:** 9-13 · **Pages:** 24 · **Size:** 8.5 × 11 in · **Activities:** Word search (7), Sudoku (5), Mazes (7), Spot the difference (5) · **Maze styles:** walls, square tube, round · **Quality:** Standard · **Credits:** 24 Standard · 48 Premium

```text
24 pages of a fossil dig in a desert canyon: 7 word searches, 5 sudoku, 7 mazes and 5 spot the differences. Paleontologists with brushes, picks and sieves, rock layers, a field tent, a museum lab. Word searches by topic: periods (TRIASSIC, JURASSIC, CRETACEOUS, MESOZOIC), dinosaurs (ANKYLOSAURUS, SPINOSAURUS, IGUANODON, DIPLODOCUS), dig site (TROWEL, SIEVE, PLASTER, CHISEL, SEDIMENT, AMBER), fossils, anatomy, sea and sky reptiles and the museum lab. Mazes run through canyon tunnels, dig-site trenches and caves.
```
