## 1. Not-so-spooky Halloween for preschoolers

**Ages:** 3-6 · **Pages:** 24 · **Size:** 8.5 × 11 in · **Activities:** Shadow matching (6), Connect the dots (6), Mazes (6), Coloring pages (6) · **Maze styles:** tube, square tube · **Quality:** Standard · **Credits:** 24 Standard · 48 Premium

```text
A cozy, not-scary Halloween activity book for ages 3-6 in 24 pages: 6 shadow-matching pages (smiling jack-o'-lanterns, a friendly ghost, a black cat in a witch hat, bats, owls, candy), 6 easy dot-to-dots numbered up to 15, 6 wide-path mazes that lead trick-or-treaters to the candy bowl, and 6 coloring pages of kids in pumpkin, bee and dragon costumes, a happy bat hanging upside down and an owl on a fence under a big round moon. Every face smiles.
```

### Page by page (24 pages)

Paste one page at a time into ChatGPT, Gemini or any AI image tool, with this line after it:

> Printable Halloween activity page for a child aged 3 to 6: friendly, smiling, not-scary cartoon characters, thick bold outlines, big simple shapes, very little background, no shading or grey; shadow-matching and maze pages in soft bright colour, dot-to-dot and coloring pages in black-and-white line art; no words except the dot numbers; portrait 8.5 x 11 page.

1. Shadow matching: match four friendly Halloween pictures to their shadows; a smiling jack-o'-lantern, a waving ghost, a black cat in a witch hat and a candy bucket on the left, their four shadows mixed up on the right.
2. Shadow matching: a happy bat hanging upside down, a round owl, a broomstick and a striped witch hat in a column on the left, each to be matched to its shadow in a jumbled column on the right.
3. Shadow matching: three costumed kids, one dressed as a pumpkin, one as a bumblebee and one as a little green dragon, to match with three shadows standing in a row below them.
4. Shadow matching: four Halloween treats to match with their shadows: a wrapped candy, a swirly lollipop, a caramel apple on a stick and a cupcake topped with a tiny pumpkin.
5. Shadow matching: four pumpkins from the patch to match by shape: a tall pumpkin, a short fat pumpkin, a pumpkin with a curly vine and a pumpkin with one big leaf, their shadows lined up beside them.
6. Shadow matching: night sky things: a big round moon with a sleepy smile, a five-pointed star, a puffy cloud and a little spider dangling on a thread, paired with four shadows to find.
7. Connect the dots 1 to 10: join the dots to draw a big round pumpkin, then a smiling jack-o'-lantern face appears; a little vine curls from its stem.
8. Connect the dots 1 to 10: a friendly ghost with a wavy bottom edge appears, waving one arm, beside a small smiling pumpkin that is already drawn.
9. Connect the dots 1 to 12: a tall pointed witch hat with a wide brim and a square buckle, sitting on the head of a sleepy, curled-up black cat.
10. Connect the dots 1 to 12: a happy bat with spread wings and a little toothy smile flies over a wooden fence, a big round moon behind it.
11. Connect the dots 1 to 15: a pumpkin-shaped candy bucket with a curved handle appears, wrapped candies and a lollipop spilling out on the ground beside it.
12. Connect the dots 1 to 15: a round owl with big eyes and pointed ear tufts perched on a bare tree branch, a crescent moon and two stars beside it.
13. Maze: help a little trick-or-treater in a pumpkin costume follow a wide path up the garden to the candy bowl; start at the garden gate, finish at the bowl on the front porch.
14. Maze: guide the friendly ghost along a wide, simple path across the pumpkin patch to its pumpkin friend; start at the smiling scarecrow, finish at the biggest pumpkin.
15. Maze: help the black cat in a witch hat find its way to its broomstick; start at the cat's round cushion, finish at the broom leaning against an apple tree.
16. Maze: lead the happy bat through a short, wide cave tunnel to its sleeping spot; start at the cave mouth with a lantern, finish at the branch where it hangs upside down.
17. Maze: help the child in a bumblebee costume walk down the street to Grandma's house for a treat; start at the lamppost, finish at the door with a pumpkin on the step.
18. Maze: guide the little kid in a dragon costume along a wide path between hay bales to the caramel apple stand; start at the farm wagon, finish at the tray of apples.
19. Coloring page: a row of three smiling jack-o'-lanterns in a pumpkin patch, one big, one middle-sized and one small, with curly vines and broad leaves around them.
20. Coloring page: a friendly ghost waving hello from behind a white picket fence, a little pumpkin at its feet and a big round moon above the fence.
21. Coloring page: a black cat in a witch hat sitting beside a bubbling cauldron, stirring it with a big wooden spoon, round bubbles floating up.
22. Coloring page: three kids in costumes, a pumpkin, a bumblebee and a dragon, walking together along the sidewalk holding their candy buckets.
23. Coloring page: a happy bat hanging upside down from a tree branch next to a round owl sitting on a fence under a big round moon.
24. Coloring page: a child in a pumpkin costume sitting on the living room rug after trick-or-treating, sorting candy into piles next to a sleepy cat.

## 2. Monster costume parade puzzles

**Ages:** 6-9 · **Pages:** 24 · **Size:** 8.5 × 11 in · **Activities:** Mazes (6), Word search (6), Spot the difference (6), Connect the dots (6) · **Maze styles:** walls, tube, round · **Quality:** Standard · **Credits:** 24 Standard · 48 Premium

```text
A neighborhood Halloween night for ages 6-9 in 24 pages: 6 mazes through a corn maze, a pumpkin patch, a spider web and the costume parade route; 6 word searches of 8 words each (PUMPKIN, COSTUME, CAULDRON, SKELETON, SCARECROW, HAYRIDE and more); 6 spot-the-difference scenes (a witch stirring soup, bobbing for apples, a parade led by a goofy three-eyed monster); and 6 dot-to-dots of a scarecrow, a dancing skeleton and a grinning jack-o'-lantern. Fun-spooky, never scary.
```

### Page by page (24 pages)

Paste one page at a time into ChatGPT, Gemini or any AI image tool, with this line after it:

> Printable Halloween activity page for a child aged 6 to 9: fun-spooky, friendly art with nothing frightening, clean medium-bold outlines, moderate detail, background only where the puzzle needs it, no shading or grey; mazes and spot-the-difference in bright colour, word search and dot-to-dot in black-and-white line art; no words except puzzle letters and dot numbers; portrait 8.5 x 11 page.

1. Maze: help three trick-or-treaters find their way through the tall corn maze to the hayride wagon; start at the scarecrow by the entrance, finish at the wagon piled with hay bales.
2. Maze: guide the goofy three-eyed monster between the rows of the pumpkin patch to the giant prize pumpkin; start at the farm gate, finish at the pumpkin with a blue ribbon.
3. Maze: a round maze shaped like a spider web on a front porch; help the little smiling spider travel from the web's outer edge to its cozy hammock in the very center.
4. Maze: lead the witch along the twisting forest path to her cottage before her soup boils over; start at the crooked signpost, finish at the cottage door with the cauldron steaming inside.
5. Maze: help the kid in a skeleton costume follow the costume parade route through neighborhood streets to the judges' stage; start at the school gate, finish at the stage hung with balloons.
6. Maze: a round maze inside a big full moon; guide the owl from the moon's edge to its nest of twigs in the middle, passing bats and stars along the way.
7. Word search: 8 Halloween night words (PUMPKIN, COSTUME, CANDY, LANTERN, GHOST, OWL, MOONLIGHT, TREAT) hidden in a 12 x 12 letter grid, framed by a row of jack-o'-lanterns and candy buckets.
8. Word search: 8 witch words (BROOMSTICK, CAULDRON, POTION, HAT, SPELL, CAT, WAND, BUBBLE) hidden in a 12 x 12 letter grid, a friendly witch stirring her cauldron in the top corner.

10. Word search: 8 harvest words (SCARECROW, HAYRIDE, CORN, APPLE, HAYSTACK, TRACTOR, VINE, HARVEST) hidden in a 12 x 12 letter grid framed by a scarecrow and tall cornstalks.
11. Word search: 8 night creature words (SPIDER, BAT, WEB, RAVEN, TOAD, WOLF, MOUSE, BEETLE) hidden in a 12 x 12 letter grid, spider webs in the corners and a crescent moon at the top.
12. Word search: 8 party words (BOBBING, APPLES, GAMES, PRIZE, CUPCAKE, MUSIC, DANCE, PARADE) hidden in a 12 x 12 letter grid framed by party balloons and a bobbing-for-apples tub.

14. Spot the difference: two pictures of kids bobbing for apples in a big wooden tub at a backyard party; find 6 changes in the apples, the party hats and the paper bunting.
15. Spot the difference: two pictures of a scarecrow in a cornfield with crows on its arms; find 6 changes, like a patch on its shirt, a missing crow and a different hat.
16. Spot the difference: two pictures of a front porch decorated for Halloween, a spider spinning a web between the posts and carved pumpkins on the steps; find 7 changes.
17. Spot the difference: two pictures of the costume parade, the goofy three-eyed monster leading kids dressed as a mummy, a pirate and a robot; find 7 changes in costumes and banners.
18. Spot the difference: two pictures of a hayride wagon pulled by a tractor under the moon, families waving from the hay bales; find 7 changes in the wagon, lanterns and stars.
19. Connect the dots 1 to 30: a witch's cauldron on three short legs appears with bubbles rising out of it, a broomstick leaning against its side.
20. Connect the dots 1 to 30: draw the goofy three-eyed monster waving hello, with two little horns and big feet, standing next to a trick-or-treat bag.
21. Connect the dots 1 to 35: a scarecrow with a floppy straw hat and patched overalls appears in the cornfield, a crow perched on one outstretched arm.
22. Connect the dots 1 to 35: a friendly skeleton in a bow tie dancing on the lawn, a carved pumpkin lantern at its feet.
23. Connect the dots 1 to 40: a big carved jack-o'-lantern with a crooked, toothy grin sitting on a hay bale, a candle inside and a curly vine on its stem.

## 3. Haunted manor puzzle book for ages 9-13

**Ages:** 9-13 · **Pages:** 24 · **Size:** 8.5 × 11 in · **Activities:** Word search (8), Sudoku (8), Mazes (8) · **Maze styles:** walls, round, square tube · **Quality:** Standard · **Credits:** 24 Standard · 48 Premium

```text
A creaky old manor on a hill on Halloween night, for ages 9-13 in 24 pages: 8 word searches of 12 words each (haunted house, creatures, library, graveyard garden, potion cellar, night sky, portrait gallery, attic), 8 sudoku from 6 x 6 warm-ups to a hard 9 x 9, framed by candelabras, cobwebs, gravestones and ravens, and 8 mazes through secret passages, hedge labyrinths, the cellar and the whole manor floor plan. Spooky, never gory.
```

### Page by page (24 pages)

Paste one page at a time into ChatGPT, Gemini or any AI image tool, with this line after it:

> Printable black-and-white Halloween puzzle page for ages 9 to 13: atmospheric, spooky-but-not-gory line art of a creaky manor, ravens and cobwebs, crisp fine-to-medium lines, a detailed decorative frame around a clear, uncluttered puzzle area, no shading or grey, no words except puzzle letters, numbers and start and finish marks; portrait 8.5 x 11 page.

1. Word search: 12 haunted house words (CANDELABRA, COBWEB, ATTIC, CELLAR, PORTRAIT, LANTERN, STAIRCASE, CHANDELIER, TRAPDOOR, FIREPLACE, CANDLE, MIRROR) hidden in a 14 x 14 grid, framed by the manor's iron gate and two stone gargoyles.
2. Word search: 12 creature words (WEREWOLF, BANSHEE, GOBLIN, VAMPIRE, ZOMBIE, PHANTOM, GHOUL, WRAITH, TROLL, OGRE, SPECTER, POLTERGEIST) hidden in a 14 x 14 grid, a raven on a gatepost and a full moon above.

4. Word search: 12 graveyard garden words (GRAVESTONE, CRYPT, IVY, RAVEN, OAK, MOSS, FOG, HEDGE, FOUNTAIN, STATUE, THORN, OWL) hidden in a 14 x 14 grid framed by crooked gravestones and a bare oak tree.
5. Word search: 12 potion cellar words (CAULDRON, BEAKER, FLASK, ELIXIR, HERBS, MORTAR, PESTLE, VIAL, SMOKE, BUBBLE, BREW, RECIPE) hidden in a 14 x 14 grid framed by shelves of corked bottles.
6. Word search: 12 night sky words (MOONRISE, ECLIPSE, COMET, CONSTELLATION, MIDNIGHT, TWILIGHT, SHADOW, STARLIGHT, HOWL, BATWING, CLOUD, TOWER) hidden in a 14 x 14 grid under the manor's tower and a crescent moon.
7. Word search: 12 portrait gallery words (FRAME, PAINTING, DUKE, DUCHESS, ARMOR, TAPESTRY, BANNER, CREST, SWORD, SHIELD, VELVET, HALLWAY) hidden in a 14 x 14 grid framed by portraits whose eyes seem to follow you.
8. Word search: 12 attic words (TRUNK, DUST, MUSICBOX, DOLLHOUSE, SPIDER, RAFTER, WINDOW, KEY, LOCKET, DIARY, CLOCK, BAT) hidden in a 14 x 14 grid framed by cobwebbed rafters and a round attic window.
9. Sudoku: a 6 x 6 warm-up grid using the numbers 1 to 6, about 14 given, with the creaky manor on its hill drawn above the grid and bats circling its tower.
10. Sudoku: a 6 x 6 grid using the numbers 1 to 6, about 12 given, framed by a twisting wrought-iron fence with a carved pumpkin on each corner post.
11. Sudoku: a 9 x 9 grid with about 36 numbers given, set at the foot of the candlelit staircase, a tall candelabra standing on each side of the grid.
12. Sudoku: a 9 x 9 grid with about 34 numbers given, set inside an ornate empty frame in the portrait gallery, a pair of painted eyes peeking over the top edge.
13. Sudoku: a 9 x 9 grid with about 32 numbers given, drawn as the page of an open spellbook on a library desk, floating books and a quill pen around it.
14. Sudoku: a 9 x 9 grid with about 30 numbers given, cobwebs stretched across the top corners and a spider dangling on a thread over the grid.
15. Sudoku: a 9 x 9 grid with about 28 numbers given, carved into a tall gravestone-shaped panel in the garden, ivy creeping up the sides and a raven perched on top.
16. Sudoku: a hard 9 x 9 grid with about 25 numbers given, framed by a full moon rising behind the manor roof and a werewolf howling on the hill below.
17. Maze: find the secret passage behind the library bookcase and follow narrow tunnels to the hidden study; start at the swinging bookcase, finish at a desk with a lit candle.
18. Maze: a round hedge labyrinth in the manor garden; walk from the iron gate on the outer ring to the stone fountain at the center, past crooked gravestones.
19. Maze: climb the candlelit staircase floor by floor; start in the great hall, finish at the attic door, dodging missing steps and suits of armor on the landings.
20. Maze: a hedge maze shaped like a giant pumpkin seen from above, wide square paths between the hedges; start at the stem gate, finish at the scarecrow in the middle.
21. Maze: wind through the cellar between barrels and potion shelves; start at the trapdoor in the ceiling, finish at the stone steps that lead up to the garden.
22. Maze: help the raven fly through the bare, twisting branches of an old oak to its nest; start at the gatepost at the roots, finish at the nest on the highest branch.
23. Maze: a round maze shaped like a spider web stretched across the attic window; start at the edge, finish at the old brass key caught in the middle of the web.
24. Maze: escape the manor before midnight; the whole floor plan seen from above with rooms and corridors; start in the attic, finish at the front door as the grandfather clock strikes twelve.
