## Tips for farm animal coloring book

- Baby animals (chicks, piglets, lambs, calves) outsell adult farm animals for the under-5 crowd; lead your cover and first pages with them.
- A named-animal book with one animal per page doubles as a first-words book, which lets you list it under early learning as well as coloring.
- Seasonal farm scenes like pumpkin patches, spring lambs and snowy barns let one farm book rank for several gift seasons.
