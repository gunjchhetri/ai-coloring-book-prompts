## 1. Eight feelings with animal friends

**Ages:** 3-6 · **Pages:** 40 · **Size:** 8.5 × 11 in · **Style:** Cute animals · **Detail:** Easy · **Layout:** One item per page, with its caption · **Quality:** Standard · **Credits:** 40 Standard · 120 Premium

```text
A first feelings book: eight feelings, each shown by animals in five moments, one per page with the feeling as its caption. Pages 1-5, happy: puppy with a new ball, piglet in mud, duckling in a bath, lamb on a swing, bear with honey. Pages 6-10, sad: bunny losing a balloon, chick's sandcastle washed away, kitten missing a friend, elephant's broken toy, frog on a rainy day. Pages 11-15, angry: bear's tower knocked over, raccoon skipped in line, goat's tangled kite, hedgehog woken early, parrot losing a game. Pages 16-18, scared: kitten in thunder, mouse in the dark, owlet's first flight.
```

**Extra direction:** Pages 19-20, scared: cub at the doctor, lamb meeting a big dog. Pages 21-25, surprised: fox opening a present, giraffe seeing snow, seal's surprise party, squirrel's giant acorn, bunny seeing fireworks. Pages 26-30, proud: duckling swimming, monkey tying a shoelace, turtle winning a race, beaver's new dam, puppy's drawing. Pages 31-35, shy: mouse meeting new friends, fawn's first school day, hedgehog behind a tree, penguin at a dance, kitten behind its mom. Pages 36-40, calm: turtle floating, koala in a hammock, cat in the sun, sloth watching clouds, bunny breathing slowly.

## 3. Calm-down toolbox

**Ages:** 6-9 · **Pages:** 40 · **Size:** 8.5 × 11 in · **Style:** General coloring · **Detail:** Easy · **Layout:** One item per page, with its caption · **Quality:** Standard · **Credits:** 40 Standard · 120 Premium

```text
A calm-down book of forty strategies, one per page with its caption, each showing a child using it when upset. Pages 1-4, breathing: smell the flower, blow out the candle; balloon breaths; tracing my hand with five breaths; bunny breaths. Pages 5-12, moving: squeezing a stress ball, wall push-ups, stretching like a cat, shaking out the wiggles, jumping ten times, a big self-hug, marching in place, rolling a ball under my feet. Pages 13-18, senses: naming five things I can see, listening to calm music, smelling a lavender bag, sipping cold water, wrapping up in a soft blanket, stroking my cat.
```

**Extra direction:** Page 19, senses: hugging a stuffed animal. Pages 20-28, quiet time: a cozy corner, reading a book, watching the clouds, watching a glitter jar, doing a puzzle, drawing how I feel, coloring a picture, writing in a journal, humming a song. Pages 29-32, thinking: counting to ten, counting back from twenty, thinking of a happy place, saying something kind to myself. Pages 33-40, people and nature: asking for a hug, talking to a grown-up, a walk with my family, going outside, watering the plants, watching fish in a tank, listening to the rain, playing with my dog.
