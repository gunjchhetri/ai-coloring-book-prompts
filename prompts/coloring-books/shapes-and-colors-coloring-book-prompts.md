## 1. Ten basic shapes, four things each

**Ages:** 3-6 · **Pages:** 40 · **Size:** 8.5 × 11 in · **Style:** General coloring · **Detail:** Easy · **Layout:** One item per page, with its caption · **Quality:** Standard · **Credits:** 40 Standard · 120 Premium

```text
A first shapes book: ten shapes, each found in four everyday things, one thing per page with the shape name as its caption. Pages 1-4, circle: a clock, a wheel, a cookie, a button. Pages 5-8, square: a window, a waffle, a picture frame, a floor tile. Pages 9-12, triangle: a pizza slice, a sail, a musical triangle, a house roof. Pages 13-16, rectangle: a door, a book, an envelope, a bed. Pages 17-20, oval: an egg, a hand mirror, an oval rug, a balloon. Pages 21-24, star: a starfish, a magic wand, a star sticker, a star lantern. Pages 25-27, heart: a heart balloon, a heart cookie, a heart card.
```

## 5. Geometry for tweens: polygons to Platonic solids

**Ages:** 9-13 · **Pages:** 40 · **Size:** 8.5 × 11 in · **Style:** General coloring · **Detail:** Hard · **Layout:** One item per page, with its caption · **Quality:** Standard · **Credits:** 40 Standard · 120 Premium

```text
A geometry book, one shape per page with its name as the caption, each shown with a real-life example. Pages 1-9, polygons by number of sides: triangle, quadrilateral, pentagon, hexagon, heptagon, octagon, nonagon, decagon, dodecagon. Pages 10-13, triangles: equilateral, isosceles, scalene, right-angled. Pages 14-19, quadrilaterals: square, rectangle, rhombus, parallelogram, trapezoid, kite. Pages 20-24, the Platonic solids: tetrahedron, cube, octahedron, dodecahedron, icosahedron. Pages 25-27, shapes in nature: honeycomb hexagons, a six-sided snowflake, a nautilus shell spiral.
```

**Extra direction:** Pages 28-32, shapes in nature: sunflower seed spirals, basalt columns, salt crystal cubes, a five-armed starfish, a spiderweb. Pages 33-40, shapes in buildings: a geodesic dome, a truss bridge, Roman aqueduct arches, a round rose window, a hexagon-tiled floor, a soccer ball of pentagons and hexagons, grain silos, an A-frame cabin.
