## Tips for unicorn coloring book

- Plain unicorn books are crowded on KDP; adding pegasus, griffins, baby dragons and fairies widens the book and the keywords you can rank for.
- Ages 4-8 is the sweet spot for unicorn books, and 30-40 pages at 8.5x11 is the format most top sellers use.
- Give every page an activity (a tea party, a carousel, a picnic) so the unicorns are doing something, not just standing in a field.
