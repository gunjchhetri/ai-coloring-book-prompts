## Tips for how plants grow coloring book

- Show the soil cut away on some pages so kids can see roots growing underground.
- Pair the book with a real seed in a clear cup; kids compare their plant to the page.
- For older kids, name the inputs and outputs of photosynthesis exactly: sunlight, water and carbon dioxide in; sugar and oxygen out.
