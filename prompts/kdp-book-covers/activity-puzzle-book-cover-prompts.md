## Tips for activity and puzzle book cover

- Show a sample puzzle on the front, like a bit of maze or a sudoku grid; it tells the shopper what kind of book it is in one glance.
- Activity books are usually printed on both sides, so sheets are half the page count.
- Use bold, saturated colors; activity book thumbnails compete with dozens of bright covers.
