These prompts set up full paperback covers for a bedtime book, an adventure about a brave little mouse and a first-day-of-school story, each with an original character. They are sized for square and letter picture books printed in color on both sides.

## Tips for children's picture book cover

- Put the main character large on the front and looking toward the story; readers bond with a face.
- A 32-page picture book printed on both sides is 16 sheets; KDP's minimum is 24 pages, which is 12 sheets.
- Choose color interior paper for picture books so the cover's spine width matches a full-color book.
