## 1. Nutmeg the kitchen mouse decorates

**Ages:** 3-6 · **Pages:** 40 · **Size:** 8.5 × 11 in · **Style:** Story · **Quality:** Standard · **Credits:** 80 Standard · 120 Premium

```text
Nutmeg, a tiny brown mouse in a red knitted hat, lives behind the kitchen wall of the Baker family's house. While the family sleeps in December, she decorates in secret: strings cranberries along the shelf, hangs a thimble bell, builds a gingerbread cottage from crumbs, wraps a walnut as a present and stitches a stocking from a sock. She rides the toy train around the tree, sledges down the banister on a spoon and hides from the cat. On Christmas morning the Baker children find a tiny stocking by the mouse hole, and they leave a crumb of cake inside for Nutmeg.
```

## 2. Thistle the reindeer trains for the sleigh

```text

Training brings take-off practice on the ice runway, a crash into the snowman orchard, loop-the-loops over the elf village, night flying by lantern, rooftop landings on the workshop and a harness relay against the older reindeer. Thistle keeps finishing last. On Christmas Eve a blizzard grounds the team, but Thistle's crooked antler catches the wind just right, and she leads the sleigh through. The book ends with her name stitched on a stall.

## 3. Wilbur the penguin and the lost letter

**Ages:** 6-9 · **Pages:** 40 · **Size:** 8.5 × 11 in · **Style:** Story · **Quality:** Standard · **Credits:** 80 Standard · 120 Premium

```text
Wilbur, a penguin postmaster in a blue cap, runs the North Pole Post Office. In December the letters pour in: by plane, by boat, by sled. He sorts them into mountains, stamps them and loads the elves' carts. On December 23 he finds one letter that slipped behind a mailbag, from a girl named Hana asking for her grandfather's lost music box to be found. Wilbur sets off by dog sled, crosses an iced-over lake, borrows a lantern from a lighthouse keeper, searches a toy-repair shop and finds the music box. He delivers it to the workshop just as the sleigh takes off.
```

## 5. Hattie runs the Christmas tree farm

**Ages:** 9-13 · **Pages:** 40 · **Size:** 8.5 × 11 in · **Style:** Story · **Quality:** Standard · **Credits:** 80 Standard · 120 Premium

```text
Hattie, eleven, in a plaid jacket and work gloves, has to run her family's Christmas tree farm for the last week before Christmas when Grandpa breaks his arm. She tags trees with families, drives the hay wagon, ties trees to car roofs, keeps the bonfire going, sells cocoa at the stand, rescues a lost dog in the pines, fixes the jammed tree baler and wraps wreaths in the barn. On Christmas Eve she and Grandpa deliver a free tree to the children's hospital and decorate it with the kids. The book ends with the family's own tree, cut last, lit up in the farmhouse window.
```
