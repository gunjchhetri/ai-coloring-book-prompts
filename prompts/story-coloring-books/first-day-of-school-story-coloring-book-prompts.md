## 1. Hazel the hedgehog starts preschool

**Ages:** 3-6 · **Pages:** 24 · **Size:** 8.5 × 11 in · **Style:** Story · **Quality:** Standard · **Credits:** 48 Standard · 72 Premium

```text
A 24-page story of Hazel, a small hedgehog with a red bow on her quills, starting preschool at Acorn Hill. She packs her lunchbox and toy beetle, walks with her mom past the pond, hugs goodbye at the gate, finds her cubby and meets her teacher, Mrs. Badger. Then come circle time, finger painting, the block corner, a berry snack, a story on the rug, nap mats, the sandbox and the slide with Dot, a mole in big glasses who becomes her friend. At pickup she runs to her mom with a painting and a plan to sit with Dot tomorrow.
```

Paste one page at a time into ChatGPT, Gemini or any AI image tool, with this line after it:

> Black-and-white story coloring page, age 3-6. Hazel: small round hedgehog, spiky quills, a bow, striped T-shirt, little backpack. Dot: small mole, big round glasses, polka-dot dress. Mrs. Badger: striped face, glasses, cardigan. Thick bold outlines, big simple shapes, little background, no shading or grey, no words in the picture, blank band at the bottom for text, portrait 8.5 x 11 page.

1. Hazel the hedgehog sitting up in her little bed, stretching her arms wide as the sun peeks through the window, her backpack waiting on a chair beside the bed.  
   *Words:* It was a big day. Hazel the hedgehog was starting preschool!
2. Hazel at the kitchen table eating a bowl of berries and a slice of toast while her mom, a taller hedgehog in an apron, pours her a cup of milk.  
   *Words:* Hazel ate her berries and toast. Her tummy felt a little wobbly.
3. Hazel kneeling on the floor packing her small backpack: a round lunchbox, a toy beetle and a pair of spare socks laid out beside it.  
   *Words:* She packed her lunchbox, her spare socks and Beetle, her very best toy.
4. Hazel standing on a stool in front of a round mirror while her mom ties a big bow onto her quills, a hairbrush on the shelf.  
   *Words:* Mom tied a bright red bow on her quills. "Ready?" asked Mom.
5. Hazel holding her mom's paw as they walk along a path past a pond, two ducks swimming among lily pads and cattails at the edge.  
   *Words:* They walked past the pond. The ducks said quack, quack, good morning.
6. Hazel and her mom arriving at Acorn Hill Preschool, a little cottage with a round door under a huge oak tree, acorns on the ground and a wooden gate in front.  
   *Words:* There was Acorn Hill Preschool, under the great big oak tree.
7. Hazel hugging her mom tightly at the open gate, her backpack on, a single tear on her cheek and her toy beetle peeking out of the pocket.  
   *Words:* Hazel hugged Mom tight. "I will come back at pickup," Mom said.
8. Mrs. Badger kneeling in the classroom doorway with a kind smile, holding out her paw to Hazel, a basket of toys just inside the room.  
   *Words:* "Welcome, Hazel," said Mrs. Badger. "We are so glad you came."
9. Hazel hanging her backpack on a hook in a row of wooden cubbies, her own cubby marked with a big acorn sticker, other cubbies with leaf and star stickers.  
   *Words:* Hazel found her very own cubby with an acorn on it. In went her backpack.
10. Circle time: a ring of small animals sitting cross-legged on a round rug, a rabbit, a squirrel and a fox clapping along while Mrs. Badger leads a song.  
   *Words:* At circle time everyone sang a clapping song.
11. Hazel sitting at the very edge of the rug hugging her toy beetle, looking shy, while Dot the mole peeks at her from behind a toy shelf.  
   *Words:* Hazel did not feel like singing yet. She held Beetle close.
12. Hazel in a big paint smock at an easel, finger painting a huge round sun, three paint pots on the easel tray and paint on her paws.  
   *Words:* Then it was painting time. Hazel painted a big yellow sun.
13. Dot standing at the next easel painting a long wiggly worm, the two of them turning to grin at each other with paint on their noses.  
   *Words:* The mole next to her painted a worm. "I'm Dot," she said. "I like your sun."
14. Hazel and Dot in the block corner stacking a tall tower of wooden blocks, the top block tipping over and both of them laughing.  
   *Words:* Hazel and Dot built a tall, tall tower. Crash! They both giggled.
15. Snack time at a small round table: bowls of berries and little cups of water, Dot's bowl tipped over and Hazel sliding her own bowl over to share.  
   *Words:* At snack time Dot spilled her berries. Hazel shared hers.
16. Hazel and Dot side by side at a low sink washing their paws, big soap bubbles floating up and a towel hanging on a hook.  
   *Words:* They washed their paws together. Bubbles, bubbles everywhere!
17. Mrs. Badger in a rocking chair reading a big picture book about a little boat, Hazel and Dot sitting close together on the rug in front of her.  
   *Words:* Mrs. Badger read a story about a little boat that was brave.
18. Nap time: Hazel lying on a mat under a small blanket, hugging her toy beetle, and Dot asleep on the next mat with her glasses folded beside her.  
   *Words:* Then it was time to rest. Hazel and Dot had mats side by side.
19. Hazel and Dot in the sandbox, Hazel filling a bucket with a shovel while Dot digs a deep tunnel with a sandcastle beside it.  
   *Words:* Outside, Hazel filled buckets and Dot dug a long tunnel.
20. Hazel whooshing down the playground slide with her arms up, Dot waiting her turn at the top of the ladder and cheering.  
   *Words:* Whee! Hazel went down the slide, and Dot came right after.
21. Clean-up time: Hazel dropping blocks into a big toy bin while Dot carries a stack of picture books back to the shelf.  
   *Words:* Everyone helped tidy up. Hazel put the blocks away.
22. Hazel running across the yard to her mom at the gate, holding her sun painting up high, Dot waving goodbye behind her.  
   *Words:* There was Mom at the gate! Hazel ran to show her the sun painting.
23. Hazel and her mom walking home past the pond holding paws, Hazel pointing at her painting as the ducks swim by.  
   *Words:* "I made a friend," said Hazel. "Her name is Dot."
24. Hazel tucked into bed with her toy beetle, her sun painting taped to the wall above her, smiling with her eyes closed under a crescent moon.  
   *Words:* That night Hazel smiled in bed. Tomorrow she would sit with Dot again.

## 3. Priya's first day at a new school

**Ages:** 6-9 · **Pages:** 24 · **Size:** 8.5 × 11 in · **Style:** Story · **Quality:** Standard · **Credits:** 48 Standard · 72 Premium

```text
A 24-page story of Priya, eight, with a long black ponytail and a backpack covered in city patches, who has just moved to the small town of Willow Creek. On her first day of third grade she rides the yellow bus, finds the office, walks into the wrong room, meets her teacher Mr. Hale and her class buddy Rosa, feeds the class snail, tries a magnet experiment, eats lunch under the big oak, plays soccer at recess, helps in the school garden and joins the library comic club. On the bus home she draws a map of her new school to show her parents.
```

Clear bold outlines, medium detail, simple background, no shading or grey, no words in the picture, blank band at the bottom for text, portrait 8.5 x 11 page.

1. Priya sitting on her bed in a new bedroom full of half-unpacked moving boxes, looking out the window at a quiet street of small houses and big trees.  
   *Words:* Priya's new bedroom was full of boxes. Out the window, Willow Creek looked very quiet after the city.
2. Priya at the kitchen table in her new house while her mom brushes her long hair into a ponytail, her dad packing a lunch bag on the counter.  
   *Words:* "First day at a new school," said Mom, brushing her ponytail. Priya's stomach did a flip.

   *Words:* In math Mr. Hale asked Priya to draw a pattern on the board. She got it right.
14. Priya and Rosa eating lunch at a picnic table under a giant spreading oak tree, sandwiches, apples and juice boxes between them.  
   *Words:* Lunch was outside under the big oak tree. Rosa traded Priya a cookie for a grape.

   *Words:* At recess the class played soccer. Priya scored a goal!

   *Words:* "You're really fast," said Rosa, puffing. Priya grinned.

   *Words:* After lunch the class worked in the school garden. Priya watered the tomatoes.

22. The end-of-day line for the buses outside the school, Priya hugging Rosa goodbye, the willow tree swaying behind them.
