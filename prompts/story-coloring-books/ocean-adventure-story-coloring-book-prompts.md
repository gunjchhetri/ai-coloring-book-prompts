## Tips for ocean adventure story coloring book

- Move the story through real ocean zones (beach, reef, kelp forest, deep sea) so every page looks different.
- Name the sea creatures in the brief: a clownfish, a manta ray, an anglerfish.
- Summer and beach-vacation buyers peak in May and June; list KDP versions at 24 pages or more.
