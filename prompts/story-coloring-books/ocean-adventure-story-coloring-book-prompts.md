## 1. Bubbles the seahorse finds her dad

**Ages:** 3-6 · **Pages:** 24 · **Size:** 8.5 × 11 in · **Style:** Story · **Quality:** Standard · **Credits:** 48 Standard · 72 Premium

```text
Bubbles, a baby seahorse with a curly tail, drifts away from her dad in a strong current on the coral reef. She sets off to find him and asks everyone she meets: a clownfish in an anemone, a sleepy sea turtle, a grumpy pufferfish, a moray eel, a family of cleaner shrimp, an octopus who changes pattern, a parrotfish crunching coral and a ray gliding over the sand. Each points her a little farther. At the seagrass meadow she finds her dad holding onto a blade of grass, waiting, and she curls her tail around his. 24 pages.
```

> Black-and-white story coloring page for a child aged 3 to 6. 1. Bubbles the baby seahorse and her dad side by side on the coral reef, both tails curled around one branch of coral, a big fan coral behind them and round bubbles rising.  
   *Words:* Bubbles was a baby seahorse. She lived on the coral reef with her dad.
2. A strong current sweeping Bubbles away from the coral, her tail unhooked and her fins flapping, swirly lines of water around her and her dad small in the distance.  
   *Words:* One day a big current came. Whoosh! It pulled Bubbles away.
3. Bubbles all alone between tall branching corals, looking left and right with wide eyes, a little spiral shell and a pebble on the sand beside her.  
   *Words:* Bubbles looked around. She could not see her dad anywhere.
4. Bubbles meeting a clownfish peeking out of a big waving sea anemone, the clownfish's stripes showing between the soft tentacles.  
   *Words:* "Have you seen my dad?" Bubbles asked a clownfish.
5. The clownfish pointing a fin past a large brain coral toward a rocky ledge, Bubbles turning to look, small reef fish swimming by.  
   *Words:* "Not today," said the clownfish. "Ask the turtle on the ledge."
6. A big sea turtle napping on a rock ledge with its eyes closed and flippers tucked in, Bubbles gently tapping its patterned shell with her snout.  
   *Words:* The sea turtle was fast asleep. Bubbles gave his shell a tap.
7. The sea turtle opening one sleepy eye and lifting a flipper to point over a sandy dip, Bubbles smiling up at its wrinkly face.  
   *Words:* "Mmm, try past the sand," yawned the turtle.
8. A grumpy pufferfish puffed up into a round spiky ball with a frown, Bubbles jumping back in surprise with her tail curled tight.  
   *Words:* Past the sand was a pufferfish. "Go away!" he puffed.
9. The pufferfish shrinking back to a small round fish with a sorry face, pointing its fin toward a crack in the rocks, a sea star on the rock nearby.  
   *Words:* Then he felt sorry. "Ask the eel in the rocks," he said.
10. A long moray eel poking its head out of a crack in a rock wall, mouth open, Bubbles bobbing politely a little way off with a small sponge below.  
   *Words:* The moray eel opened his big mouth. "Ask the shrimp," he said.
11. A family of cleaner shrimp on a round rock, one shrimp cleaning inside the open mouth of a big calm grouper while Bubbles watches.  
   *Words:* The shrimp were busy cleaning a grouper's teeth.
12. Three little cleaner shrimp waving their long feelers at Bubbles, all pointing the same way toward a ring of rocks and shells.  
   *Words:* "Go that way," they said, and pointed with their feelers.
13. An octopus sitting in a ring of rocks with stripes on some arms and spots on the others, Bubbles staring at the patterns with her mouth open.  
   *Words:* That way was an octopus who could change her stripes to spots!
14. The octopus giving Bubbles a ride on the curl of one arm, lifting her over a tall rock wall, a few bubbles drifting up.  
   *Words:* "Hold on," said the octopus, and lifted Bubbles over the wall.
15. A parrotfish with a beak-like mouth crunching on a lump of coral, tiny crumbs of sand falling down, Bubbles watching from behind a sea fan.  
   *Words:* Crunch, crunch! A parrotfish was eating coral.
16. The parrotfish and Bubbles swimming together over smooth white sand, a sand dollar and a little conch shell on the bottom below them.  
   *Words:* "I make sand," said the parrotfish. "Follow it to the ray."
17. A wide flat ray gliding over the sand like a kite, Bubbles holding on with her tail to the edge of its wing, sand ripples underneath.  
   *Words:* The ray let Bubbles ride along. Swoosh, over the sand!
18. The ray landing softly on the sand to let Bubbles off, a starfish and a scallop shell beside it, tall green blades of seagrass ahead.  
   *Words:* "Your dad likes the seagrass," said the ray. "It is just there."
19. Bubbles resting her tail on a little rock and yawning, very tired, a small crab tiptoeing past and a snail on a nearby pebble.  
   *Words:* Bubbles was very tired. But she did not give up.
20. Bubbles at the edge of the seagrass meadow, peeking between the tall waving blades of grass, small fish darting in and out.  
   *Words:* She came to the seagrass meadow. The grass was so tall!
21. Bubbles weaving between the seagrass blades, a sea snail climbing one blade and a tiny shrimp hiding behind another.  
   *Words:* Bubbles swam between the blades. "Dad? Dad!"
22. Bubbles' dad holding onto a single blade of seagrass with his tail, looking out with a worried face, waiting, a few bubbles above him.  
   *Words:* And there was Dad, holding onto the grass, waiting for her.
23. Bubbles curling her tiny tail around her dad's big tail, both hugging with eyes closed and smiling, round bubbles all around them.  
   *Words:* Bubbles curled her tail around his. "I found you!"
24. Bubbles and her dad holding tails among the seagrass while the clownfish, sea turtle, pufferfish, octopus and ray gather around to wave.  
   *Words:* All her new friends came to say hello. Bubbles was home.

## 2. Juno the humpback calf's migration

**Ages:** 6-9 · **Pages:** 24 · **Size:** 8.5 × 11 in · **Style:** Story · **Quality:** Standard · **Credits:** 48 Standard · 72 Premium

```text
Juno, a humpback whale calf with one white flipper, is born in a warm lagoon. In spring she and her mother swim north thousands of miles to the cold feeding grounds. On the way Juno practices breaching, races spinner dolphins, rests over a coral bank, passes a container ship, hides from orcas behind her mother, sees sea lions on a buoy, swims under a fishing boat, hears other whales singing and crosses the open ocean in a storm. In the cold waters she sees icebergs and krill, and joins the adults blowing bubble nets to feed. 24 pages.
```

24. Juno resting full and sleepy beside her mother at the surface, snowy mountains and a low evening sun behind them over the calm cold sea.

## 3. Finn's message in a bottle

**Ages:** 6-9 · **Pages:** 24 · **Size:** 8.5 × 11 in · **Style:** Story · **Quality:** Standard · **Credits:** 48 Standard · 72 Premium

```text
Finn, eight, in a striped life jacket, finds a bottle on the beach with a hand-drawn picture map inside. He and his grandpa sail their small boat, the Pelican, to follow it. They stop at a lighthouse island, a sandbar full of seals, a mangrove with a heron's nest, a reef they snorkel over, a cove with a shipwreck and a fishing village where an old woman recognizes the map: she drew it as a girl seventy years ago. The last mark leads to a tin of her childhood treasures buried under a palm. Finn adds his own drawing and they bury it again. 24 pages.
```
