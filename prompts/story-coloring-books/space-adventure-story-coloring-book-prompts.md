## Tips for space adventure story coloring book

- Make each stop a real place (the Moon, Mars, Saturn's rings) so the book teaches a little as it tells the story.
- Bring the hero home at the end; a trip that returns feels finished.
- For KDP, 24 pages is the minimum; a planet-by-planet tour easily fills 30.
