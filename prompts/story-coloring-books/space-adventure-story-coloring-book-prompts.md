## 1. Luna-Mae's teapot rocket to the Moon

**Ages:** 3-6 · **Pages:** 24 · **Size:** 8.5 × 11 in · **Style:** Story · **Quality:** Standard · **Credits:** 48 Standard · 72 Premium

```text
Luna-Mae, a small mouse in a knitted helmet, wants to know if the Moon is made of cheese. In 24 pages she builds a rocket from a teapot, a thimble and two spoons, blasts off from the kitchen windowsill, floats past clouds, a satellite and a sleepy comet, lands in a crater, bounces in low gravity, finds old astronaut footprints and a parked moon buggy, digs in the dust, watches Earth rise and licks a moon rock: not cheese. She flies home, lands in the sugar bowl and has cheese on toast for supper.
```

### Page by page (24 pages)

Paste one page at a time into ChatGPT, Gemini or any AI image tool, with this line after it:

> Black-and-white coloring page, ages 3 to 6. Luna-Mae is a small cartoon mouse with big round ears, a thin tail and a knitted pom-pom helmet; her rocket is a round teapot with a thimble nose and two spoon fins; same look on every page. Thick bold outlines, big shapes, little background, no shading, no grey, no words in the picture, blank band at the bottom for text, portrait 8.5 x 11 page.

1. Luna-Mae the little mouse standing on the kitchen windowsill at night, gazing up at a big round full Moon, a wedge of cheese and a flowerpot beside her.  
   *Words:* Luna-Mae the mouse looked up at the big round Moon. "I think it is made of cheese," she said.
2. Luna-Mae tucked into her matchbox bed with a picture book about the Moon open on her lap, a candle stub glowing on a button table.  
   *Words:* She could not stop wondering. There was only one way to find out for sure.
3. Luna-Mae pushing an old round teapot across the kitchen counter with both paws, a thimble and two spoons waiting in a pile beside the bread bin.  
   *Words:* She found an old teapot, a silver thimble and two shiny spoons.
4. Luna-Mae on a step of cotton reels tying two spoons to the sides of the teapot with string, the thimble set on top of the lid like a pointed nose.  
   *Words:* She tied on the spoons for fins and put the thimble on top. Her rocket was ready!
5. Luna-Mae pulling on her knitted helmet with a pom-pom and packing a cracker into a tiny backpack, her teapot rocket standing on the windowsill behind her.  
   *Words:* Luna-Mae put on her knitted helmet and packed a cracker for the trip.
6. Luna-Mae waving from the open teapot lid on the windowsill, one paw raised, the window open and the curtains tied back.  
   *Words:* She climbed in and counted down. Three, two, one...
7. The teapot rocket blasting off out of the open kitchen window, big puffy clouds of steam underneath and the curtains flapping.  
   *Words:* Whoosh! The teapot rocket blasted off out of the window.
8. The teapot rocket zooming up through fluffy clouds, a surprised bird flapping beside it and Luna-Mae waving from the spout window.  
   *Words:* Up she flew, past the fluffy clouds and one very surprised bird.
9. The teapot rocket gliding past a satellite with wide square solar panels and a round dish, little stars dotted around them.  
   *Words:* She waved to a satellite turning slowly in the dark.
10. A sleepy cartoon comet with closed eyes and a long swishing tail yawning as the teapot rocket zips past, Luna-Mae giggling at the window.  
   *Words:* A sleepy comet gave a big yawn as she zoomed by.
11. Luna-Mae floating upside down inside the round teapot rocket, her tail curled, cracker crumbs and her backpack floating around her.  
   *Words:* Inside the rocket, Luna-Mae floated upside down. Her cracker crumbs floated too!
12. Luna-Mae pressing her nose to the round window as the huge Moon fills the view, covered in big and small round craters.  
   *Words:* The Moon grew bigger and bigger. It was covered in round holes.
13. The teapot rocket landing on its spoon fins in the middle of a big round crater, a puff of moon dust rising around it.  
   *Words:* Bump! The rocket landed in a big round crater.
14. Luna-Mae climbing down a little rope ladder from the teapot and stepping onto the dusty Moon, leaving her first tiny footprint.  
   *Words:* Luna-Mae climbed down and made her very first tiny footprint.
15. Luna-Mae bouncing high above the Moon's surface with her arms spread wide and her tail streaming, round rocks and craters far below.  
   *Words:* On the Moon she could jump so high! Boing, boing, boing!
16. Luna-Mae standing inside one giant old astronaut boot print, a long trail of the same huge prints stretching away across the dust.  
   *Words:* She found giant old footprints. Someone had been here before!
17. Luna-Mae sitting in the big seat of a parked moon buggy with four wire wheels and a round antenna, her feet dangling far above the floor.  
   *Words:* Next to them was a parked moon buggy. She sat in the big seat and pretended to drive.
18. Luna-Mae digging in the soft moon dust with a teaspoon, a small pile of dust and a thimble bucket beside her, sniffing the air.  
   *Words:* She dug in the dust with a spoon. It did not smell like cheese at all.
19. Luna-Mae sitting on a moon rock watching the round Earth rise over the edge of the Moon, swirls of clouds on it and stars all around.  
   *Words:* Then she saw Earth rising over the edge of the Moon. Home looked beautiful.
20. Luna-Mae licking a lumpy moon rock and pulling a funny scrunched-up face, her tongue out and her ears flat.  
   *Words:* At last she licked a moon rock. Yuck! The Moon is not made of cheese.
21. The teapot rocket flying back toward the big round Earth, the Moon getting smaller behind it, Luna-Mae laughing at the window.  
   *Words:* Luna-Mae laughed, climbed back into her rocket and flew home.
22. The teapot rocket landing nose-first in the sugar bowl on the kitchen table, sugar cubes flying up and a milk jug beside it.  
   *Words:* Plop! She landed right in the sugar bowl.
23. Luna-Mae at a table made from a cotton reel eating a slice of cheese on toast, her knitted helmet hanging on a nail behind her.  
   *Words:* For supper she had cheese on toast. Earth cheese is the best cheese of all.
24. Luna-Mae fast asleep in her matchbox bed, the teapot rocket parked by the wall and the full Moon smiling in through the window.  
   *Words:* That night the Moon shone in at her window, and Luna-Mae smiled in her sleep.

```text

In 24 pages they launch from the school field, skim hot Mercury, peek under Venus's clouds, race a dust storm on Mars, weave through the asteroid belt, circle Jupiter's Great Red Spot, slide along Saturn's rings, pass tilted Uranus and ride Neptune's winds.
